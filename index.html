Get started

In under a minute.

Three steps and you're spawning in.

  1. Open Minecraft Java Edition and go to Multiplayer.
  2. Click Add Server and paste mc.cedrick105.com as the address.
  3. First time? Type /register <password> <password>. After that, just /login <password>.
